Strength calibration

Strength–maturity relationship

Strength is estimated as S = a + b · ln(M) with M in °C·h. Obtain a and b from compressive tests of your own mix. Until both are set the dashboard shows “Calibration required”.

Demo values are placeholders to preview the display. They are not validated for any concrete mix.
